# F1.8 — Acoustic Out-point Snapping & ASR Over-estimation Correction

**Status:** planned
**Depends on:** F1.6, F1.7
**Difficulty:** 7/10
**Layer:** Audio analysis / EDL boundary refinement / QC validation
**Requirement:** CUT-02

## Problem Statement

ASR aligners (such as ElevenLabs Scribe or Whisper models) frequently suffer from **timestamp over-estimation** on phrase endings: the timestamp of the last word is extended across 500ms to 2000ms of subsequent silence or speaker breath before the next sentence.

In previous implementations, `helpers/refine_edl_boundaries.py` enforced a rigid clamp:
```python
if t_offset < float(last_word["end"]):
    t_offset = float(last_word["end"])
```
This clamp prevented the VAD / RNNoise acoustic decay from snapping the cut to the actual physical end of speech, forcing the rough cut to retain hundreds of milliseconds of dead air (as observed in timeline cut `00;00;38;01`, where word `"crescer."` ended physically at `85.10s` with `-55 dBFS` silence, but ASR reported `85.82s`, trapping 640ms of dead zone).

Conversely, ASR under-estimation can cut words prematurely (e.g. cutting sibilant sounds in words like `"confiança"`).

## In Scope

1. **Acoustic Out-point Decoupling:**
   - Detect the true acoustic offset (`c_end`) of the active speech component containing `last_word["start"]`.
   - When bilateral evidence (`activity_raw` and `activity_rnn`) proves speech energy dropped below noise threshold ($\le -45\text{ dBFS}$) into sustained silence before `last_word["end"]`, allow `t_offset` to snap to `c_end` without clamping to `last_word["end"]`.
2. **Standard +2 Frames Cadence:**
   - Calculate `F_out = time_to_frame(c_end, fps, "ceil") + 2` (~66ms natural air), adhering to the jump-cut cadence rule.
3. **Under-estimation Protection:**
   - When active speech component continues past `last_word["end"]` (`c_end > last_word["end"]`), extend `t_offset` to `c_end` to preserve final consonants/syllables.
4. **Collision & Neighbor Guarding:**
   - Ensure `F_out` never invades the next rejected word frame limit `F_next_limit`.
5. **QC / Readiness Integration:**
   - Update `helpers/verify_edit_ready.py` to validate out-points that are earlier than `last_word["end"]` when accompanied by valid acoustic decay evidence.
6. **Synthetic Unit Tests:**
   - Add unit tests in `tests/test_acoustic_outpoint_snap.py` covering:
     - Long ASR tail with clean early acoustic decay (over-estimation recovery);
     - Premature ASR end with lingering acoustic tail (under-estimation recovery);
     - Collision with closely-following rejected words;
     - Idempotent re-runs on refined EDLs.

## Out Of Scope

- Modifying upstream ASR provider models.
- Changing editorial selection logic in Step 06.
- Adding arbitrary visual fades or audio gain normalization.
